## 1. CoEvoBench: Can LLMs Drive Recursive Co-Evolution of Agentic Systems?

`相似度 4.197` ｜ Xander Xu ｜ Zhejiang University; Alibaba Group; Shanghai Jiao Tong University

**研究问题**：现有基准要么只评模型侧训练、要么在固定模型下优化 harness，未能度量 LLM 研究者能否递归协同进化目标智能体的模型与 harness。

**方法**：构建覆盖五域智能体任务的基准：研究者从共同基座模型与初始 harness 出发，在24小时预算内迭代，提交的系统在独立测试集评分。

**结果与证据**：五个前沿 LLM 担任研究者，最强者缩小与参考系统差距逾五分之四；交互类任务成功而长程编码少见，早期强改进后难将反馈转为下一轮增益。

**方向关联**：以协同进化（进化计算概念）评估 LLM 智能体递归自改进，直接契合 LLM 与进化计算交互这一重点关注方向，也贴合科研素养主线。

**代码复现线索**：论文写明实验设置：任务按可见/选择/测试约1:2:2划分，目标为两个不同家族的基座模型，每研究者每域运行三次。

<summary>原始摘要</summary>

> The performance of an LLM agent depends on both its model and its harness, the code that mediates its interaction with tools and environments. Can LLM researchers drive this co-evolution as a recursive improvement process, deciding round after round which component to change when each change alters the value of the other? Existing benchmarks evaluate post-training or data research with the harness out of scope, or harness optimization with the model fixed, leaving this joint decision unmeasured. We introduce CoEvoBench, a benchmark that evaluates LLMs as researchers co-evolving the model and harness of a target agent across five agentic domains. Researchers start from a common base model and initial harness, train and revise within a 24-hour budget, and submit a model-harness pair that is scored on separate test tasks. Across five frontier researchers, the strongest closes more than four fifths of the gap to an instruction-tuned reference system, and co-evolution succeeds on interaction tasks but rarely on long-horizon coding. These results suggest that current LLMs can make a strong first improvement, but cannot yet turn feedback from an improved system into the next one. CoEvoBench provides a controlled testbed for measuring the research capabilities that recursive co-evolution requires.

## 2. LEAN: Library-Based Adaptation for Asynchronous, Federated Fine-Tuning

`相似度 3.819` ｜ Chris Jermaine ｜ Rice University; Amazon AGI Lab

**研究问题**：跨设备联邦微调基础模型时,本地LoRA适配器因置换不可辨识且数据异质,平均聚合不适用。

**方法**：服务器维护秩1基对库,客户端随机借出子集本地LoRA微调后归还,基对洗牌流转,无聚合、全异步,仅传低秩参数。

**结果与证据**：同步设定下证明收敛到稳定点邻域;图像与文本基准多数设置优于平均基线、通信更省,重异质与重异步下仍有效。

**方向关联**：与LLM支线交集具体:基础模型联邦LoRA微调与通信效率;与进化计算主线关联较弱,库式随机借还或可作群体式思路对照\(解释性\)。


<details>
<summary>原始摘要</summary>

> Federated fine-tuning of foundation models with low-rank adapters (LoRA) is typically reconciled by averaging locally trained adapters. In cross-device settings, where participants hold heterogeneous data and join or leave asynchronously, averaging is poorly suited: locally trained adapters are not identifiable up to permutation, and averages of adapters trained on dissimilar data can be uninformative or unstable. We propose a simple idea called federated library-based adaptation (LEAN), which replaces averaging altogether. The server maintains a library of rank-1 basis pairs; participants check out random subsets, fine-tune them locally as a LoRA adapter, and check them back in, so that each pair is shuffled across sites and trained on many participants' data. Because no aggregation step is required, LEAN operates fully asynchronously and communicates only low-rank parameters. We prove that, in a synchronous setting, LEAN converges to a neighborhood of a stationary point, with the shuffle canceling data heterogeneity at first order. Across image and text fine-tuning benchmarks, LEAN outperforms averaging-based baselines in most settings with substantially less communication, and remains effective under heavy data heterogeneity, where averaging-based methods fail to reach nontrivial accuracy, and under heavy asynchrony, where they degrade substantially.

</details>

## 3. MAPES: Multi-Value Alignment Pipeline via Expert Specialization

`相似度 3.799` ｜ SangKeun Lee ｜ Korea University

**研究问题**：如何在LLM偏好对齐中同时满足多个相互冲突的人类价值，克服单模型共享参数空间中的跨价值冲突。

**方法**：提出MAPES四阶段管线：按价值分数据路由、价值专用LoRA专家的间隙加权DPO训练、偏好感知合并PAVM、合并后协调。

**结果与证据**：五个偏好对齐基准平均分超DPO变体与多目标基线，GSM8K/GPQA无大幅退化，对照实验隔离各阶段贡献。

**方向关联**：契合LLM支线（对齐与偏好优化）；多目标冲突主题与进化计算的多目标优化仅概念呼应，未涉及LLM×进化计算交互。

**代码复现线索**：论文给出实现要点：基于UltraFeedback数据路由并平衡至22k偏好对，各价值训练LoRA适配器，PAVM用每价值128对校准集估计方向。


<details>
<summary>原始摘要</summary>

> Aligning large language models (LLMs) with human preferences requires simultaneously satisfying multiple, often competing values. Existing preference optimization methods collapse these into a single implicit signal, and even multi-objective extensions optimize within a shared parameter space, leaving cross-value conflicts unresolved. We propose MAPES, a multi-value alignment paradigm in which conflict resolution is a first-class concern at every pipeline stage: it first decomposes supervision into value-specific experts trained in isolated parameter subspaces, then recomposes them via preference-aware value merging and a lightweight harmonization stage that aligns the merged model with the expert ensemble. Across five preference alignment benchmarks, MAPES outperforms strong direct preference optimization (DPO) variants and multi-objective baselines on the benchmark-level average, with no large degradation on GSM8K and GPQA; matched controls isolate the contribution of each stage.

## 4. Reliable Behavior Discovery via False Discovery Rate Control

`相似度 3.695` ｜ Utsav Prabhu

**研究问题**：如何在大模型行为发现中控制错误发现率\(FDR\)，以保证发现的行为模式可靠。

**方法**：提出DISCO：用粗化e过程做迭代搜索，自适应确定待检验假设数量，在温和条件下可证明控制FDR。

**结果与证据**：理论证明FDR可控；在合成数据及大模型兼任发现引擎与对象的实验中，功效高于eBH等对比方法。

**方向关联**：契合科研方法主线与LLM支线：为LLM行为发现提供假设检验与FDR控制的严谨方法论；与进化计算关系弱。


<details>
<summary>原始摘要</summary>

> Reliable methods for discovering behavior patterns in large generative models are increasingly essential as models become both more complex and capable. Building upon the multiple hypothesis testing literature, we propose DISCO (Discovery through Iterative Search with COarsened $e$-processes), a method which provably controls FDR in behavior discovery under mild conditions. DISCO improves power over previous related eBH-based approaches by allowing for adaptivity to a data-dependent number of hypotheses to be tested. We demonstrate empirically that DISCO controls FDR with higher power than comparable approaches on both synthetic data and a novel empirical evaluation of FDR-controlling methods in settings where large generative models act as both discovery engines and targets. ## 5. Improving the Optimisation Landscape for Particle Mixtures

`相似度 3.610` ｜ Chris J. Oates ｜ Monash University; Newcastle University; Imperial College London

**研究问题**：混合预测似然不适于梯度优化，会使失配粒子得不到训练信号（责任饥饿），需修正该优化目标。

**方法**：证明混合风险的缺陷，提出个体风险与混合风险的凸组合正则化，在粒子责任上施加 reverse-KL 屏障。

**结果与证据**：MFLD 案例仅在正则化下数值良好；四个 LLM 骨干、八个多选推理基准上正则化估计均得更低 held-out 混合风险。

**方向关联**：与 LLM 支线直接相关（LoRA 集成微调 LLM）；多粒子联合优化的信号分配与群智算法概念交叉，契合 LLM 与进化计算交互关注。

**代码复现线索**：正文明确：LoRA 集成在四个骨干、八个多选推理基准上联合微调，采用带噪声的 AdamW 变体更新。


<details>
<summary>原始摘要</summary>

> Several methods in statistics and machine learning represent a predictive distribution as an equally weighted mixture of models, or particles, trained via the mixture predictive likelihood. However, we prove that the mixture predictive likelihood is poorly suited to gradient-based optimisation, because it does not identify how the mixture is decomposed across particles, so that a poorly matched particle can be starved of training signal. As a remedy, we study a convex combination between the individual risk, i.e. the average log loss of the individual particles, and the mixture risk. At the particle level, this regularised risk imposes a reverse-KL barrier on the responsibilities of the particles, so that no particle can be starved of training signal. These phenomena are illustrated in two case studies: In the first, a predictively-oriented posterior is computed using mean-field Langevin dynamics, and the numerics are well-behaved only when our regularisation is employed. In the second, ensembles of low-rank adapters are jointly fine-tuned on large language models, across four backbones and eight multiple-choice reasoning benchmarks; direct minimisation of the mixture risk leads to responsibility starvation, whose severity decreases as the competence of the backbone increases, whereas our regularised estimator attains a lower held-out mixture risk on every backbone considered.

</details>

## 6. Many Plans at Once: Diverse Top-Quality Planning in Continuous Time

`相似度 3.605` ｜ Buser Say ｜ Monash University

**研究问题**：连续时间规划中如何预先获得低成本且彼此可区分的多条轨迹，供未建模约束出现时替换或热启动。

**方法**：MPAO采样输出天然满足边界条件的未训练神经网络，训练共享修正，存储全部已验证轨迹并自适应采样，具anytime性。

**结果与证据**：等预算对比五基线，MPAO唯一解出四域全部40实例；接触丰富操作上唯一达到最优成本25%以内，此类计划数约为最强基线十倍。

**方向关联**：与进化计算支线有具体重叠：其维护多样解集合、按已发现结果自适应采样，近似质量多样性搜索思想；摘要未涉LLM。


<details>
<summary>原始摘要</summary>

> Planners for physical systems usually return a single trajectory, which leaves no alternative when an unmodeled constraint invalidates the chosen route or downstream preferences change. A set of plans computed in advance provides ready-made alternatives and warm-starts for fast adaptation, but only if the plans are both low-cost and mutually distinct. Discrete planning addresses this with top-quality and diverse planning, but these ideas do not transfer to continuous time, where every valid trajectory has infinitely many valid near-copies, and existing trajectory planners converge to one or a few solutions. We present Many Plans at Once (MPAO), which treats every plan it finds as a reason to search elsewhere: it samples untrained neural networks whose outputs meet the boundary conditions by construction, trains corrections shared by networks from the same distribution, stores every validated plan, and adapts its sampling to what it has found, which makes it anytime. We define the goal by bringing the cost bound of top-quality planning and the distinctness threshold of diverse planning to continuous time. Under equal budgets against five baselines, MPAO is the only method that solves all 40 instances across four domains. On contact-rich manipulation, it is the only method with plans within 25% of the best known cost on one task, and returns about ten times as many such plans as the strongest baseline on the other two.

</details>

## 8. Loop-OPF: Solver-Informed Learning for Optimal Power Flow with Inference-Time Topology Adaptivity

`相似度 3.530` ｜ Hao Zhu ｜ The University of Texas at Austin

**研究问题**：现有学习法求最优潮流难以保证预测的最优性与可行性，且推理时难以适应线路或发电机停运等未见拓扑。

**方法**：提出 Loop-OPF：将 PDHG 迭代展开为网络层，用 DEQ 框架自监督学习 PDHG 参数，拓扑作为每层输入以直接适应新场景。

**结果与证据**：作者称在 IEEE 300 母线及 PEGASE 1354/2869/9241 母线网络上的数值测试中，准确性与拓扑适应性优于对比学习方法。

**方向关联**：与画像关联弱：论文属电力系统最优潮流的求解器展开学习（PDHG/DEQ），不涉及进化计算、群智算法、LLM/API 或多智能体方向。

**代码复现线索**：文中仅声明在 IEEE 300 母线与 PEGASE 1354/2869/9241 母线网络上测试，未提供训练或实现细节。


<details>
<summary>原始摘要</summary>

> Learning for optimal power flow (OPF) has recently been advocated to accelerate OPF solutions by using a pre-trained neural network (NN) to predict directly from given inputs such as the load demand. However, existing learning-for-OPF methods largely fall short of ensuring the optimality and feasibility of their predictions. They also struggle to adapt at inference time to unseen topology scenarios, such as those caused by line or generator outages. This work puts forth Loop-OPF, a solver-informed NN that builds its layers from the iterations of the primal-dual hybrid gradient (PDHG) method. Under standard convexity and regularity conditions on DC-OPF, the resultant fixed-point output of Loop-OPF is thus optimal and feasible. To speed up PDHG across diverse OPF scenarios, Loop-OPF learns certain PDHG parameters in a self-supervised manner, without any OPF solution labels. We adopt the deep equilibrium (DEQ) framework for training so that the memory cost does not grow with the number of layers. As the grid topology enters every layer as an input, the same trained Loop-OPF directly adapts to unseen contingencies without retraining. Numerical tests on the IEEE 300-bus and PEGASE 1354-, 2869-, and 9241-bus networks have demonstrated the accuracy and topology adaptivity of the proposed Loop-OPF over competing learning-for-OPF methods.

</details>

## 9. Keep the Process, Change the Supplier: Runtime Governance for Tool-Using AI Agents

**研究问题**：研究如何在运行时治理工具型智能体的能力调用，使授权、上下文披露与供应商选择独立于模型输出，并处理供应商故障、不确定结果与替换。

**方向关联**：与LLM/API支线部分重叠：涉及智能体工具/API调用的运行时治理与供应商路由，可为智能体集成外部能力提供参考；但论文不评测LLM，也无关进化计算。

<details>
<summary>原始摘要</summary>

> Tool-using AI agents propose actions whose authorization and external effects cannot be inferred from generated text alone. We study an execution boundary that separates agent proposals from business-controlled authority, context disclosure, and supplier selection. The Open Process Control Plane (OPCP) mediates capability calls and records uncertain outcomes when a supplier may have acted without returning an acceptable reply. We evaluate this runtime with scripted requests and synthetic suppliers: 2,400 fault episodes across four policies, 60 process-termination/restart episodes, 19,000 timed calls across 19 configurations, five targeted mutations over 43 fixtures, and 160 substitution records. No language model or autonomous agent is evaluated. OPCP and a conservative composition agree on all measured fault outcomes. These results characterize an execution mechanism relevant to agent governance, including its costs and assumption boundaries. They do not establish improved agent task success, prompt-injection resistance, or real-vendor model portability.

## 10. MedicalRSI: Medical Recursive Self-Improving Agents via Clinical Model-Harness Co-Evolution

`相似度 3.511` ｜ Kaize Ding

**研究问题**：医疗LLM智能体部署后固定不变、同类错误跨患者复发；现有自改进方法的改进策略固定，难以实现递归自改进。

**方法**：提出MedicalRSI：单模型兼任Solver（医疗智能体）与Improver（可学习改进策略），依失败写医疗harness，按相对无harness的增益联合训练两角色。

**结果与证据**：论文称在多个基准与LLM骨干上优于上下文级与权重级自改进方法，且可迁移到未见工作流、新LLM与新数据库。

**方向关联**：契合LLM/API支线的智能体自改进方向；其"模型-harness协同进化"思想与进化计算存在概念交集，正对应LLM与进化计算交互的关注点。


<details>
<summary>原始摘要</summary>

> Medical agents built on large language models (LLMs) have advanced rapidly, yet most stay fixed once deployed and do not keep using their experience to improve later attempts. As a result, the same kind of error can recur across patients. To address this, existing self-improvement methods update an agent's weights or its medical harness from its own experience, but their improving strategy stays fixed and never learns which changes actually help, which caps how much the agent can improve. Recursive self-improvement (RSI) instead improves both the agent and its improving strategy. However, achieving RSI for medical agents is challenging, because clinical workflows rarely come with a reference harness, and a change that fixes early errors may not fix the problems the agent meets later. We propose MedicalRSI, an RSI framework built on Clinical Model-Harness Co-Evolution, in which one model plays two roles. The Solver is the medical agent itself, and the Improver is its learnable improving strategy, which writes a medical harness from the agent's failures. Each harness is rewarded by its gain over acting without it, and both roles are trained together, so the Improver keeps adapting to the problems the Solver has not yet solved. On multiple benchmarks and LLM backbones, MedicalRSI outperforms existing context-level and weight-level self-improvement methods and transfers well to unseen workflows, new LLMs and new databases, a step toward medical agents that keep improving.

</details>

We investigate whether a learned activation function can also be transferred and remain useful in networks trained from scratch. We study this complementary form of transfer through a learn-freeze-transfer protocol, in which the activation is optimized jointly with a source network in a single training run, and is then frozen and reused in target networks trained from scratch. A pointwise activation with globally shared parameters is independent of layer dimensions and can therefore be incorporated into networks of different architectures trained from scratch. We instantiate this protocol with FAct, a five-parameter activation represented by a two-harmonic truncated Fourier series, initialized from a Fourier approximation of GELU and learned in a Vision Transformer (ViT) on ImageNet-1K. When reused in a ViT of the same architecture trained from scratch on ImageNet-1K, the frozen activation (Frozen FAct) improves top-1 accuracy by 1.46 percentage points over GELU. Across four smaller target architectures (standard ViT, Swin Transformer, linear-attention ViT, and MLP-Mixer) and four image-classification datasets, it achieves the highest mean accuracy among eleven activation functions in 15 of 16 settings under a common training configuration, with improvements of up to 1.71 percentage points over the best conventional activation. When the learning rate and weight decay are tuned separately for each activation under the same search budget, Frozen FAct still ranks first in 11 of the 16 settings, second in four, and fourth in one. These results provide empirical evidence that a learned activation function can retain its usefulness when transferred across vision architectures, independently of the weights with which it was learned.
