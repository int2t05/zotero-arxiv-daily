**研究问题**：LLM智能体能否在固定算力下，为多种优化问题在未见过的大规模实例上设计出可行、优质且快速的算法。

**代码复现线索**：文中给出数据细节：每题含公开实例、私有计分实例、可行性检查器与Gurobi参考解，测试阶段每题限时60秒。

<summary>原始摘要</summary>

> Operations research (OR) relies on algorithms that experts tailor to each problem: a new routing, scheduling or network design problem can take weeks of specialist work before it is solved well at scale. Large language model (LLM) agents now write, test and improve code, but whether they can design algorithms that scale to realistic instances is open: in the FrontierOR benchmark's one-shot evaluation, the strongest model matches Gurobi, with an objective within 1% of Gurobi's and no slower, on only 39% of the large instances. ## 2. Beyond Weight Updates: Evolving Agent Harnesses for Domain Specialization

`相似度 4.382` ｜ BoHao Chen ｜ Independent Researcher

**研究问题**：研究在不更新LLM权重的前提下，通过进化智能体harness（系统提示、工具模式、技能库、规划脚手架）实现领域专门化。

**方法**：将harness进化建模为RL式外循环，用GEPA反思式进化优化器搜索提示、工具、技能等基因型，策略冻结，并与SAO权重更新路径对比。

**结果与证据**：三基准显示权重更新单路径增益最强，harness进化以小部分算力恢复大部分增益，组合最佳；另指出SFT会缩短推理链并增加截断。

**方向关联**：高度契合：GEPA进化优化直接作用于LLM智能体harness，正对应画像中LLM与进化计算交互的重点，也属LLM/API支线的智能体方向。

**代码复现线索**：正文明确实验设定：Codev编码智能体在Docker沙箱（50轮、32k上下文、30分钟）中于三基准运行四种配置。


<details>
<summary>原始摘要</summary>

> Domain specialization of LLM agents is usually framed as a weight-update problem: collect trajectories, run reinforcement learning (RL) or supervised fine-tuning (SFT), and deploy a new checkpoint. We study a complementary axis: evolving the \textbf{agent harness}---the system prompt, tool schemas, skill library, and planning scaffolding---while keeping the policy frozen. We cast harness evolution as an RL-style outer loop and instantiate it with GEPA, a reflective evolutionary optimizer. The resulting harness adapters require no GPU training, are loadable per vertical domain, and compose with weight-based RL. Experiments with Codev, a Claude Code-style coding agent, on SWE-bench Verified, OfficeBench, and Terminal-Bench show a consistent pattern: weight updates give the strongest single-path gains, harness evolution recovers most of those gains at a small fraction of the compute, and combining both paths is best overall. We also analyze a failure mode of SFT-based specialization---shortened reasoning chains and increased truncation---and argue that harness adapters avoid it by construction. Our results position harness evolution as a practical, non-destructive complement to weight updates for vertical agents.

</details>

## 5. HapBO: Harnessed $\\alpha$-$\\pi$ Bayesian Optimization via Expert Natural Language Priors

`相似度 3.873` ｜ Jean-S\\'ebastien Giroux, Rose Guay Hottin, Marco Bonizzato

**研究问题**：如何将仅以自然语言存在的专家或 LLM 先验稳健地融入贝叶斯优化，以提升样本效率。

**方法**：提出 HapBO，将专家或 LLM 一次性给出的信念编译为可复用信念函数 π，以有界正乘子作用于非负采集函数，优化中零 LLM 调用。

**结果与证据**：理论证明对 EI 即使信念对抗仍保持简单遗憾率（至有界因子）；HPOBench 40 任务聚合遗憾最低，真实任务 12-20 次查询内改善。

**方向关联**：契合画像中 LLM 与优化的交互：LLM 一次性提供先验且环内零调用（关联 API 支线），贝叶斯优化与进化计算同属黑盒优化。

**代码复现线索**：摘要写明评测含 40 个 HPOBench 任务、6-15 维合成套件与两个真实应用；所供正文无实现或数据细节。


<details>
<summary>原始摘要</summary>

> Bayesian Optimization (BO) can be substantially more sample-efficient when informed by prior knowledge about the problem. In practice, however, such knowledge is often available only in natural language, e.g., expert intuitions or documentation, rather than as the explicit probability distribution landscape that prior-guided BO methods may require. Leveraging it effectively demands both an expressive mapping from language to priors and robustness to information that may be misleading. We propose HapBO (Harnessed $\alpha$-$\pi$ Bayesian Optimization), which elicits beliefs once from a human expert, a large language model (LLM), or both, and deterministically compiles a structured specification into a reusable belief function ($\pi: \mathcal{X} \to[0,1]$), with a rich representation not restricted to a Gaussian family. Building on $\alpha$-$\pi$BO, HapBO applies this belief function through a bounded positive multiplier on any nonnegative acquisition, preserving positive acquisition support and requiring no LLM calls during optimization. We prove that, for the Expected Improvement acquisition function (EI), HapBO preserves the standard simple-regret rate up to a bounded factor, even under adversarial beliefs. We evaluate HapBO on 40 HPOBench tasks, a synthetic suite in 6-15 dimensions and two real-world applications: cortical neurostimulation and constrained solar design. HapBO achieves the lowest aggregate HPOBench regret and strong real-world task performance, with zero in-loop token cost. In real-world applications it improves the optimization outcome even at budgets of 12-20 queries, and at a small fraction of the wall-clock and token costs of other LLM-based methods.

## 6. Compiling Constraints into Neuromorphic Ising Machines

`相似度 3.859` ｜ Faiek Ahsan, Shantanu Chakrabartty ｜ Washington University in St. Louis

**研究问题**：约束组合优化中，单自旋翻转伊辛机只能靠能量罚项处理约束，导致势垒升高、罚强难调且迭代浪费在不可行态。

**方法**：提出以可行多自旋移动取代罚项的高阶神经形态伊辛机，编译器将奇偶、基数、one-hot、划分与置换约束多项式规模地编译为连接布线。

**结果与证据**：论文称三阶图匹配所需迭代比罚项式机器少数个量级；FPGA 采样器 52μs/shot 达码距 15 最大似然译码，噪声症状下实时译码至码距 9 且优于置信传播。

**方向关联**：与主线（LLM×进化计算、群智算法）无直接交集，关系较弱；可行态移动设计可类比元启发式邻域算子与约束处理，仅具方法学参考价值。

**代码复现线索**：所供正文明确提及 UltraScale+ FPGA 采样器、表面码症状由仿真生成、图匹配采用 WILLOW-ObjectClass 数据集；文本未说明仓库复现细节。


<details>
<summary>原始摘要</summary>

> Most practical combinatorial optimization problems involve constraints, and an Ising machine that flips one spin at a time can respect them only through energy penalties that raise barriers between feasible solutions. Here we show that a higher-order neuromorphic Ising machine can enforce constraints through programmable transitions rather than penalties. In an autoencoder-like architecture, each inner-layer neuron represents a move, a family of multi-spin transitions that maps every feasible state to another feasible state, receives the energy change of its move through connections that outer-layer neurons multiplicatively switch on or off, and updates the state through the same connections. A compiler turns constraints into this wiring with a number of auxiliary clauses that grows only polynomially for parity, cardinality, one-hot, partition and permutation constraints. Restricting the search to feasible states reduces constraint handling to move design, and we show that the fastest-annealing moves are not those that mix fastest but those that lower the deepest energy barriers, and that moves chosen from the objective lower them further than random ones. On third-order graph matching, the machine needs orders of magnitude fewer iterations than penalty-based neuromorphic machines. Configured as a sampler on a field-programmable gate array, it decodes the surface code with maximum-likelihood accuracy up to code distance 15 within 52 $\mu$s per shot and, under noisy syndrome measurements, decodes in real time for millisecond measurement rounds up to distance 9 with fewer logical errors than belief-propagation decoders.

## 7. PWLNet: A Framework for Linearized Neural Surrogates in Nonlinear Process Optimization

`相似度 3.813` ｜ Sunoy Sanyal, Canberk Demir, Metin T\\"urkay, Erdal Ayd\{\\i\}n ｜ Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute; Koç University; Aalto University

**研究问题**：神经网络代理嵌入数学优化时，平滑激活会引入非线性非凸约束，导致难以高效求解。

**方法**：提出 PWLNet 框架：统一物理信息训练、优化感知剪枝，及 ReLU MILP、精确 tanh、PWL-tanh、SOSX 的 Pyomo 嵌入。

**结果与证据**：剪枝 15% 使 Williams-Otto 的 MILP 求解提速 6.5 倍；Van de Vusse 真实厂收益提升 4%；加氢甲酰化达参考产量 99.2%。

**方向关联**：关系较弱：论文属过程系统工程与混合整数优化，无进化计算、群智算法或 LLM 内容，仅与炼化背景松散相关。

**代码复现线索**：论文称其为开源 Python 框架，统一覆盖训练、剪枝与嵌入工作流，支持自定义 PWL 断点数与位置及 SOSX 训练，含四个基准案例。


<details>
<summary>原始摘要</summary>

> Neural networks represent nonlinear process systems accurately, but embedding them in optimization is difficult because smooth activations introduce nonlinear, nonconvex constraints. PWLNet is a Python framework for building optimization-compatible neural surrogates through exact and piecewise-linear reformulations. It combines baseline and physics-informed training, optimization-aware pruning, and Pyomo embeddings based on ReLU mixed-integer linear models, exact tanh nonlinear models, PWL-tanh formulations, and binary-free SOSX branch-and-LP. PWL-native training uses the same activation in training and embedding, avoiding extra linearization error. It is evaluated on Boston housing, the Williams-Otto process, the Van de Vusse reactor, and a hydroformylation semi-batch reactor. On Boston housing, SOSX training gave the best common-architecture model (test MAE 1.984, R2 = 0.925). On Williams-Otto, pruning 15% of hidden neurons reduced MILP solution time 6.5-fold while preserving the selected operating point. For the Van de Vusse reactor, a physics-informed ReLU RNN pruned from 32 to 12 hidden units enabled tractable exact MILP embedding and a 4% true-plant economic gain over the best ramp-and-hold policy, while terminal-aware shrinking-horizon prediction reached 99.7% of the direct-shooting reference. For the hydroformylation reactor, one-step exact-tanh IPOPT reached 99.2% of the certified reference product with peak pressure 20.002 bar (limit 20 bar) and 14.36 s total solve time over 280 moves; ReLU and PWL-native controllers achieved comparable recovery, with ReLU fastest. Longer horizons and physics-informed training did not consistently improve closed-loop results. Overall, compact linearized neural surrogates enable accurate, tractable optimization in steady-state and dynamic applications, with constraint fidelity identified as a key direction for improvement.

</details>

## 8. Fast-JEPA: Advancing the Success-Latency Frontier for Latent World Models

**方法**：Fast-JEPA 先由世界模型引导生成目标导向提案，再用直接终端预测比较完整候选，避免候选评估中的递归状态 rollout。

**方向关联**：关系较弱：主题为世界模型视觉规划，不涉 LLM；搜索中局部 CEM 采样与群体类优化思路略有交集，可作边缘参考。

**代码复现线索**：正文给出 PushT、Reacher、Cube、TwoRoom 四环境与 DynaGoal 三协议基准及搜索预算扫描，但未见实现与训练细节。


<details>
<summary>原始摘要</summary>

> Latent world models predict the outcomes of action sequences, but comparing candidate plans through recursive rollout remains expensive at test time. We introduce Fast-JEPA, a latent world model planning framework that advances the success-latency Pareto frontier of predictive search. Fast-JEPA reorganizes predictive computation to support effective action-sequence search at low planning cost. It constructs a goal-directed plan to guide search and directly predicts the outcomes of complete candidates for efficient comparison, avoiding recursive state rollout during refinement. Across PushT, Reacher, Cube, and TwoRoom, Fast-JEPA achieves 96.50% average success rate with 2.41x faster planning than INTACT. Fast-JEPA achieves average success rates of 73.83% on DynaGoal-Shift and 87.67% on DynaGoal-Deadline, demonstrating the practical value of low-latency predictive planning under changing control conditions and execution-time constraints. Our code is available at https://github.com/Sofilyzia/Fast-JEPA.

## 9. ROAM-GRPO: Enforcing Reasoning Diversity in GRPO via Routed Off-mode Approach Mining

`相似度 3.787` ｜ Jeff Duan, Matt Thomson ｜ Caltech; Living Intelligence

**研究问题**：SFT+RLVR 后训练使 LLM 陷入推理模式坍缩、pass@k 停滞，论文研究如何在 GRPO 中强制推理多样性。

**方法**：检测高解率问题并转入离模轨道，促使模型产出不同解法，经 LLM 判官验证有效且相异后自蒸馏进策略。

**结果与证据**：训练 Qwen3.5-2b 后峰值 pass@16 提升约 4.7%、正确解嵌入余弦距离约增 1.5–2 倍，但 pass@1 低于 GRPO 基线。

**方向关联**：属 LLM 后训练/RLVR，契合 LLM/API 支线；组内多样性探索与进化计算多样性思想相通，但论文未提进化计算。

**代码复现线索**：文中给出：Qwen3.5-2b 配 LoRA（r=8，α=16），MATH L5 千题子集，G=8、H=16、λ=0.2、无 KL，判官 Qwen3.5-9b。


<details>
<summary>原始摘要</summary>

> Supervised fine-tuning (SFT) paired with Reinforcement Learning with Verifiable Rewards (RLVR) has become the dominant recipe for post-training LLMs on complex reasoning tasks, but has been shown to collapse the policy into a low-diversity mode. Repeated LLM outputs are nearly identical, and pass@k accuracy converges or degrades as a result. We propose ROAM-GRPO (Routed Off-mode Approach Mining for GRPO), an augmentation to the GRPO algorithm which incentivizes exploration of distinct reasoning strategies and aims to increase output creativity during evaluation. ROAM-GRPO detects well-solved problems during training and transfers them to an "off-mode" track. The model is prompted to produce distinct solution strategies to such problems, which are verified for logical validity and distinctness by an LLM judge and subsequently self-distilled into the policy. Training Qwen3.5-2b on a 1,000-problem subset of the MATH lvl5 dataset, ROAM-GRPO implementation increases peak pass@16 by 4.7% and correct embedding cosine distance by approximately 1.5-2x on a difficult evaluation set.

</details>

## 99. ADAPT: Adaptive Decision and Abstention with Pool-Size-Aware Thresholds for Service Routing

`相似度 3.008` ｜ Manile Srun, Muntasir Adnan, Arslan Shaukat, Carlos C. N. Kuhn ｜ University of Canberra

**研究问题**：检索式路由分类器在无合适服务时仍强制选择候选，且固定弃权阈值随候选池规模 K 增大而系统性失准。

**方法**：提出 ADAPT：以最高分与候选分数分布的间隔为置信度，用随池规模 K 自适应的阈值实现弃权，无需重训分类器。

**结果与证据**：在两个专家数据集、多种检索模型和递增服务数下的实验表明，K 感知阈值相对生成式 LLM 路由取得有效的精度-成本权衡。

**方向关联**：与 LLM/API 支线具体重叠：面向服务的轻量查询路由并与生成式 LLM 路由对比；与进化计算主线无直接关联。


<details>
<summary>原始摘要</summary>

> Retrieval-based classifiers efficiently route queries to specialised services, but they typically select a candidate even when no suitable service exists. We introduce Adaptive Decision and Abstention with Pool-Size-Aware Thresholds for Service Routing (ADAPT), a lightweight routing architecture with an explicit fallback option that enables queries to abstain from specialist selection. ADAPT uses the separation between the highest relevance score and the candidate score distribution as routing confidence. Experiments across retrieval models and increasing numbers of services reveal a consistent empirical behaviour: the confidence distribution changes systematically with candidate-pool size K, making fixed abstention thresholds increasingly miscalibrated. ADAPT addresses this effect with a K-aware threshold that adapts as services are added without classifier retraining. Evaluation across two specialist datasets shows that this simple mechanism provides an effective accuracy-cost trade-off compared with generative LLM routing, supporting lightweight and dynamically extensible service-routing architectures.

## 100. Beam Model Technical Report

`相似度 3.003` ｜ Reflection Team, Adam Abdulhamid, Osama Abuelsorour, Yasmin Alameddine, Carlo Alfano, Duarte Alves et al. ｜ Reflection AI

**研究问题**：构建面向代码、推理与智能体任务的稀疏MoE大模型，兼顾前沿推理能力与更低推理算力。

**方法**：预训练23.8T token并经两阶段中期训练获得1M上下文；后训练为10.5K GPU上的大规模异步RL。

**结果与证据**：报告称能力随RL算力增长无平台期，有害请求拒绝/转向率88.9%、无害请求合规率81.5%。

**方向关联**：属LLM/API支线的前沿大模型报告，可作API选型参考；未涉及LLM与进化计算的结合。


<details>
<summary>原始摘要</summary>

> Beam is a sparse Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) model with 501.3B total parameters and 23.4B active parameters, specifically built and optimized for coding, reasoning, and agentic workloads. Beam delivers frontier level reasoning, while requiring 3-4x less inference compute than many models of its class. Pretrained on 23.8T diverse, curated, high-quality tokens, Beam features a 1M-token context window achieved through a novel two-stage midtraining phase, built on verifiable, correctness-anchored data. Post-training leveraged a large-scale, fully asynchronous reinforcement learning (RL) campaign across 10.5K GB300 GPUs, generating over 100M rollouts over 4 weeks of training. We believe this is one of the largest scale RL runs conducted by any open lab to date. Across our evaluation suite, capabilities continued to improve as we increased RL compute, with no sign of a plateau. Supported by deep hardware-model co-design, FP8/NVFP4 quantization, and a 1.93B-parameter speculative decoding drafter (DFlash 2), Beam maximizes serving throughput with efficient, flexible deployment. Averaged across conversation turns and harm categories, Beam refuses or pivots on 88.9% of harmful requests while complying with 81.5% of harmless requests, achieving the highest refusal/pivot rate on harmful requests among open-weight models on the Pareto frontier.

</details>
