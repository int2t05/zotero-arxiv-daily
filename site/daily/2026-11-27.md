## 9. Efficient Data Fusion in Distributionally Robust Optimization

`相似度 3.721` ｜ Buse \\c\{S\}en, Aras Selvi, Jean Pauphilet, Daniel Kuhn ｜ London Business School; UCL; EPFL

**研究问题**：研究分布鲁棒优化中多源碎片化证据的融合：按交集组合多个模糊集时，精确重构随源数指数增长，交集非空判定为NP难。

**方法**：将融合DRO精确重构为仅经函数拉格朗日乘子耦合的形式，再把乘子限制为仿射决策规则，得到可分解、可证明更紧的上界。

**结果与证据**：投资组合、报童与二分类实验显示仿射近似接近精确值、扩展性远优于精确方法、样本外优于聚合式基准。

**方向关联**：与研究主线关系较弱：论文核心是分布鲁棒优化的多源证据融合，不涉及进化计算或LLM，仅神经网络训练应用与机器学习略有交集。


<details>
<summary>原始摘要</summary>

> Decisions under uncertainty increasingly rest on fragmented evidence, such as samples from related but different populations, privacy-protected summary statistics, or complementary statistical descriptions of the same data. Distributionally robust optimization (DRO) can fuse such evidence by constructing one ambiguity set per source and hedging against all distributions in the intersection of these sets. This fusion approach is less conservative than aggregating the sources into a single ambiguity set, but it is computationally demanding. Exact reformulations grow exponentially with the number of sources, and even deciding whether the intersection is nonempty is NP-hard. We show that fusion DRO admits an exact reformulation in which the ambiguity sets are coupled only through functional Lagrange multipliers. Restricting these multipliers to affine decision rules yields upper bounds that decompose across the ambiguity sets, are exact for quadratic losses over Wasserstein balls around Gaussian references, and provably dominate aggregation-based relaxations such as Minkowski sums and balls around mixtures. The approximations admit finite conic reformulations in important special cases and subgradient methods for weakly convex losses, which makes fusion DRO applicable to neural network training. In portfolio, newsvendor, and binary classification experiments, the affine approximation stays close to the exact value wherever the latter is computable, scales far better than exact methods, and outperforms aggregation-based benchmarks out-of-sample.

</details>

## 10. Geometry, masking and selection in Differential Evolution and SOMA on quantum state manifolds

`相似度 3.709` ｜ Vojt\\v\{e\}ch Nov\\'ak, Ivan Zelinka ｜ IT4Innovations National Supercomputing Center; VSB–Technical University of Ostrava; Klaipėda University

**研究问题**：DE与SOMA依赖向量算术，但周期角、球面、量子态流形等非欧空间使减法与掩码失真，需几何一致的算子设计。

**方法**：固定算子与选择规则，仅替换几何原语（差分构造、掩码、选择），在教科书流形和变分量子问题上开展大规模配对数值实验。

**结果与证据**：几何一致差分使CR=1的DE在31个非欧配置中16个显著更优；周期同步成功率44%升至100%，六量子比特变分问题平均损失降90%且仅增约3%运行时间。

**方向关联**：与进化计算支线直接契合：系统研究DE/SOMA核心差分、掩码与选择算子在不同几何下的设计规则；与LLM/API支线无明显关联。


<details>
<summary>原始摘要</summary>

> Differential Evolution (DE) and the Self-Organizing Migrating Algorithm (SOMA) generate new candidates through vector arithmetic: they subtract population members, scale the difference, and mask coordinates. Many practical search spaces are not vector spaces, however. Rotation angles are periodic, normalized states lie on spheres and projective spaces, and circuit parameters are coupled through the quantum states they produce. We present a systematic study of how such geometry affects the core operations of DE and SOMA. Keeping the operators and selection rules fixed, we replace only their geometric primitives and separate the effects of difference construction, masking, and selection in a large paired numerical study covering textbook manifolds and variational quantum problems. Geometry-consistent differences make full-crossover (CR = 1) DE significantly better on 16 of 31 non-Euclidean configurations, with no significant deterioration, and the advantage persists when the measured computational cost of each variant is taken into account. The effect is largest where ordinary subtraction misrepresents distances between candidates: success on periodic synchronization rises from 44% to 100%, and on a positive-definite matrix problem from 1% to 99%. On six- qubit variational eigensolvers, shortest-path angular differences reduce the mean final loss by 90% for about 3% extra runtime. The improvements come with two contrasting diversity patterns, more effective population contraction or preserved population diversity, so higher acceptance rates do not necessarily indicate better search. Crossover masks interact with geometry: they can favor cheaper retractions and can increase the distance moved in quantum-state space when circuit parameters are coupled. These results give practical rules for designing population-based optimizers on non-Euclidean and quantum search spaces.

**方法**：提出与队伍规模无关的聚合观测架构，将单一训练策略复制到不同规模队伍实现零样本行为重构，并以预训练策略热启动重训。

**结果与证据**：四智能体队训练的策略部署到3-8队：3、5队性能基本保持；热启动重训减少至多90%训练回合；6、8队可按指令有效分配空间区域。

**方向关联**：多智能体协同与跨规模策略迁移与分布式多智能体辅助阅读方向相关，但方法为深度强化学习，非进化计算或LLM，关联有限。

<summary>原始摘要</summary>

> Although multi-agent deep reinforcement learning enables coordinated behavior, aligning it with managerial intent remains difficult due to complex agent interactions. Although the strategy-following distributed attentional actor architecture (sfDA7-X) improves controllability via regional instructions, instruction combinations scale exponentially with team and instruction sizes, leading to prohibitive training costs and poor transferability. To reduce this computational cost, we introduce a team-size-independent observation architecture and reconstruct collective behavior by replicating a single trained policy across agents in teams of different sizes, thereby eliminating the need to train from scratch. Additionally, we evaluate warm-start retraining by initializing policy networks with a pre-trained policy to significantly lower computational overhead. In an object collection game, a policy trained in a four-agent team is deployed to teams of three to eight agents. Experiments demonstrate that training with the proposed aggregated observation matrix achieves performance comparable to that of the baseline method, sfDA7-X. Furthermore, policy replication preserves performance without substantial degradation in three- and five-agent teams, and warm-start retraining outperforms training from scratch while reducing required training episodes by up to 90%. Deployments to six- and eight-agent teams further confirm effective spatial region allocation following given instructions. These results demonstrate that the proposed method enables zero-shot collective behavior reconstruction via policy replication.

## 100. $\\mu$MPC: Microsecond-Scale Nonlinear and World Model Control from Edge to GPU via Koopman-Linearized Precompilation

`相似度 3.094` ｜ Devesh Nath, Glen Chou ｜ Georgia Institute of Technology

**研究问题**：如何利用学习的Koopman线性化动力学削减非线性MPC在线计算量，实现从微控制器到GPU的微秒级实时控制与规划。

**方法**：离线预计算控制-输出映射得到仅含动作的KC-QP，在线为只需矩阵向量乘与投影的原始-对偶迭代，并以可微KC-QP和DAgger式聚合训练动力学。

**结果与证据**：论文报告桌面CPU求解低至23μs、Crazyflie机载24ms、4096维四足控制\<5ms、200架四旋翼GPU上\>30Hz，最高较对比方法快108×与1907×。

**方向关联**：与进化计算及LLM均无直接关联；仅多智能体并行规划与分布式多智能体辅助阅读方向有边缘交集，总体相关性弱。

<summary>原始摘要</summary>

> We present $\mu$MPC, a fast model predictive control (MPC) framework that reduces online computation using learned Koopman-linearized dynamics. By precomputing finite-horizon control-to-output maps, $\mu$MPC eliminates lifted-state trajectories, yielding an action-only Koopman-Compiled QP (KC-QP) with $Hn_u$ decision variables, independent of lifted dimension. A fixed, precomputed primal operator enables efficient primal-dual iterations using only matrix-vector products and projections, accommodating changing goals and constraints without refactorization. This efficiency also enables control-aware dynamics learning through differentiable KC-QPs in the training loop and batched DAgger-style data aggregation. $\mu$MPC achieves solve times as low as $23\mu$s on a desktop CPU and 24 ms onboard a 168 MHz Crazyflie microcontroller. It scales to 4096D quadruped control in under 5 ms, visual planning with 2048D world models in 0.4 ms, and collision-aware planning for 200 quadrotors at over 30 Hz on the GPU. Across these benchmarks, $\mu$MPC achieves up to $108\times$ faster constrained MPC and $1907\times$ faster visuomotor planning than competing methods.
