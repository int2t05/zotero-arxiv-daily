## 1. AscendMOEA: An Ascend NPU-Accelerated Platform for Tensorized Multi-Objective Evolutionary Algorithms

`相似度 4.748` ｜ Qianlong Dang, Zhiyang Zhang, Baosheng Li, Tianqing Zhu, Junhu Ruan ｜ City University of Macau; Northwest A&F University

**研究问题**：如何在昇腾NPU等加速器上高效执行多目标进化算法，同时保持原算法的选择顺序与更新语义不变。

**代码复现线索**：实现基于PyTorch与TorchNPU/CANN，NPU端统一float32；验证要求索引、前沿编号、平局决策与评估次数精确一致。

<summary>原始摘要</summary>

> Multi-objective evolutionary optimization increasingly involves large populations, expensive problem evaluations, and diverse algorithmic structures. These workloads expose the limited parallelism of loop-based implementations, while rewrites for accelerator execution can alter selection order, tie handling, and population updates. AscendMOEA is presented as a scalable platform that executes multi-objective evolutionary algorithms (MOEAs) on Huawei Ascend neural processing units (NPUs) and conventional central processing units (CPUs). ## 100. Accelerated Reinforcement Learning for Soft Robotics: Hours Instead of Years

`相似度 3.264` ｜ Roshan S. Kaundinya, Hehui Zheng, Robert K. Katzschmann, George Haller

**研究问题**：软体机器人硬件难以获得大量交互数据或可靠仿真器，需要为强化学习训练构建高效的替代环境。

**方法**：利用软体动力学的时间尺度分离，从预采集数据学习六维绝热谱子流形多项式ODE，作为强化学习训练环境。

<summary>原始摘要</summary>

> Reinforcement learning (RL) requires large amounts of interaction data or a reliable simulator, both hard to obtain for nonlinear soft robotic hardware. Recent work shows that a time-scale separation inherent to soft robot dynamics justifies their reduction to low-dimensional invariant manifolds, known as adiabatic spectral submanifolds (aSSMs). We demonstrate that an aSSM-reduced model, a simple polynomial ordinary differential equation learned purely from pre-collected data, can serve as an RL training environment. On a pneumatically actuated soft arm, we train a trajectory-tracking policy in 3 hours on a six-dimensional aSSM-reduced model and deploy it without fine-tuning, whereas reproducing this policy training extrapolates to 3 years of hardware interaction. Our policy tracks dynamic targets with 40% lower error than otherwise identical policies trained on a 4.8-million-parameter recurrent neural network (RNN) or a 1.7-million-parameter transformer, which take 13 and 100 hours to identify, against 5 minutes for our mathematically justified 6,924-parameter model.
