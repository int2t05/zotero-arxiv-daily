## 1. From Recursion to Convolution: Parallel Self-Improvement for Multi-Agent Systems

`相似度 4.266` ｜ Zheng Wang, Yuang Liu, Sachith Abeywickrama, Yangkai Ding, Tao Yu, Xinyu Zhang et al. ｜ Huawei Technologies Co., Ltd.

**研究问题**：多智能体自我改进中串行递归易传播误差、推高成本并限制并行，孤立精炼单个特征又忽视特征间依赖。

**方法**：提出ConvRSI：智能体内一维卷积联合精炼相关特征，智能体间图卷积按协作拓扑聚合消息，均在隐空间并行更新。

**结果与证据**：作者报告MATH-500上较RecursiveMAS性能提升5.04%，推理成本降17.27%，时延降45.11%。

**方向关联**：契合LLM支线与辅助阅读的多智能体协同进化方向，可作LLM与进化计算交互的参考，但核心方法为卷积而非进化算法。

**代码复现线索**：正文称在数学与编码两类基准（含MATH-500）评测，使用多模型家族及顺序、混合等协作模式。

<summary>原始摘要</summary>

> Multi-agent self-improvement enables agents to improve through interaction and feedback. However, sequential dependencies across agents and refinement rounds can propagate errors, increase computational costs, and limit parallelism. Moreover, refining individual features or agents in isolation can overlook their dependencies and restrict collective coordination. To address these challenges, we propose ConvRSI, a framework for parallelizing multi-agent self-improvement through convolution. ConvRSI couples intra-agent convolution, which jointly refines related agent features, with inter-agent graph convolution, which aggregates messages according to the collaboration topology. Both stages operate in latent space, enabling parallel updates without repeatedly decoding and re-encoding intermediate messages. Experiments on MATH-500 show that ConvRSI achieves 5.04% performance improvement while reducing 17.27% inference cost, and 45.11% latency compared with RecursiveMAS. It also supports diverse collaboration patterns by representing their topologies as adjacency matrices. These results demonstrate the potential of convolutional co-evolution for efficient multi-agent self-improvement. Our code and datasets are provided in https://github.com/LYA2333/ConvRSI.

## 2. Evaluation-Free Recursive Self-Improvement for Coding Agents

`相似度 4.257` ｜ Zhe Ren, Guowei Rong, Yimeng Chen, Nanbo Li, Piotr Pi\\k\{e\}kos, Mateusz Ostaszewski et al. ｜ King Abdullah University of Science and Technology \(KAUST\); Jilin University

**研究问题**：研究编码智能体能否在进化循环内不依赖下游任务评估实现递归自改进，以降低评估成本并避免面向固定基准的过拟合。

**方法**：提出ELITE：每代从同一父代并行实现多样聚焦改进，检查所得程序识别互补、冗余与冲突机制，将兼容机制整合为一个后代；循环内仅用确定性与运行有效性检查。

**结果与证据**：在SWE-bench Pro 60任务子集上从60.0%升至85.6%，零候选任务评估，耗时较DGM\(86.7%\)与HGM\(88.3%\)分别降8.8倍与3.2倍；跨基准迁移平均73.3%与69.3%。

**方向关联**：与“LLM与进化计算交互”重点方向直接对应：以进化式探索—整合机制实现LLM编码智能体自改进，核心问题是进化循环中的评估成本与选择信号。

**代码复现线索**：文中明确：固定LLM仅修改scaffold源码，在SWE-bench Pro 60任务子集上评估并对照DGM/HGM，另在SWE-bench Verified与Polyglot上测试零适配迁移。


<details>
<summary>原始摘要</summary>

> Recent self-improving coding agents have achieved strong benchmark performance by iteratively modifying their own executable implementations. A common pattern uses downstream task-derived evidence to decide which candidates or lineages continue evolving, which we term evaluate-and-select. Obtaining this signal requires repeatedly executing evolved agents on downstream tasks, so broader exploration incurs increasing evaluation cost, while repeated optimization against a fixed evaluation distribution may encourage benchmark-specific adaptation. We ask: can coding agents self-improve without downstream task evaluation inside the evolution loop? We introduce Explore-then-Integrate (ELITE), which integrates implementation mechanisms rather than selecting a single exploratory child to continue the lineage. At each generation, ELITE proposes diverse focused improvements, implements them independently from the same parent, and inspects the resulting programs to identify complementary, redundant, and conflicting mechanisms before reimplementing a compatible subset as one coherent descendant. No downstream task outcomes or task-solving trajectories of evolved candidates are used to guide evolution; only deterministic checks of structural and operational validity are used inside the loop. On SWE-bench Pro, ELITE improves the initial agent from 60.0% to 85.6%, compared with 86.7% for the Darwin Godel Machine (DGM) and 88.3% for the Huxley-Godel Machine (HGM), while using zero candidate task evaluations and reducing observed wall-clock evolution time by 8.8x and 3.2x, respectively, under the evaluated configurations. In cross-benchmark transfer without further adaptation, ELITE averages 73.3% on SWE-bench Verified and 69.3% on Polyglot. Together, these results demonstrate the feasibility of coding-agent self-improvement without downstream candidate task evaluation in our setting.

## 3. MOBO-CAPS: Multi-Objective Bayesian Optimization with Cardinality-Aware Pareto Selection

`相似度 4.144` ｜ Hanyang Wang, Juergen Branke, Matthias Poloczek ｜ University of Warwick; Databricks

**研究问题**：将多目标贝叶斯优化形式化为MOBO-CAPS问题：在有限评估预算下直接寻找恰好M个高超体积解，而非先近似整个Pareto前沿再事后筛选。

**方法**：提出两种基数感知采集策略：替换感知的期望超体积改进REHVI，以及通过联合UCB超体积目标构造乐观M点集合的CHO-UCB。

**结果与证据**：论文证明批量CHO-UCB具有次线性简单遗憾界，并在九个基准（含合成与真实应用）上实验显示基数感知采集优于标准MOBO加事后子集选择。

**方向关联**：与进化计算有概念交集：超体积与Pareto子集选择同为进化多目标优化核心议题，文中综述涉及求解HSSP的进化算法；但论文本身属贝叶斯优化，与LLM/API及多智能体方向无明显关联。


<details>
<summary>原始摘要</summary>

> Multi-objective Bayesian optimization (MOBO) traditionally aims to approximate the entire Pareto front, but, due to resource constraints, real-world deployment is often limited to testing a small, representative subset of solutions. Hence current methods first approximate the full front before selecting solutions, potentially wasting costly evaluations. We formalize this problem as Multi-objective Bayesian Optimization with Cardinality-aware Pareto Selection (MOBO-CAPS), where the goal is to identify a set of exactly M solutions with high hypervolume. We propose two practical cardinality-aware acquisition strategies: REHVI, a replacement-aware approximation to expected hypervolume improvement, and CHO-UCB, which constructs an optimistic M-point set by optimizing a collective UCB-based hypervolume objective. We prove a sublinear simple regret bound for the batched version of CHO-UCB, and empirically show that cardinality-aware acquisition design improves over standard MOBO followed by post-hoc subset selection across nine benchmarks.

</details>

## 4. Inequality-Constrained Optimization via Sliding Mode Control

`相似度 4.123` ｜ Rawan Hoteit, Veronica Centorrino, Efe C. Balta, John Lygeros

**研究问题**：求解带不等式约束的优化问题，目标是允许不可行初始化并具收敛保证的连续时间方法。

**方法**：将原始变量梯度流与由平滑Fischer-Burmeister流形上滑模控制律导出的乘子动力学耦合，并给出抗数值刚性的离散算法。

**结果与证据**：证明轨迹可从任意原始初点有限时间到达流形并化为对数障碍梯度流，强凸问题指数收敛；在多个机器学习应用中验证精度与高维可扩展性。

**方向关联**：关联较弱：论文属控制论视角的约束优化理论，与进化计算/群智算法、LLM/API两条支线及多智能体方向均无直接重叠，仅优化基础可作背景。


<details>
<summary>原始摘要</summary>

> We present COSMIC (Constrained Optimization via Sliding Mode for Inequality Constraints), a continuous-time solver for inequality-constrained optimization. COSMIC couples primal gradient-flow dynamics with multiplier dynamics derived from sliding mode control laws on a manifold defined by the smoothed Fischer-Burmeister function. We prove that COSMIC's trajectories reach this manifold in finite time from any primal initial point, after which the dynamics reduces to the gradient flow of a logarithmic barrier function. This yields a control-theoretic interpretation of barrier methods while allowing infeasible initialization. For strongly convex problems, we further establish exponential convergence to the minimizer. To address the numerical stiffness that arises in discretization, we develop a modified discrete-time algorithm. We validate COSMIC across various machine learning applications, demonstrating its ability to solve optimization problems accurately while scaling effectively to high-dimensional settings.

</details>

## 8. PISG: Constraint-Aligned Signal Amplification for Diffusion-Based Combinatorial Optimization

`相似度 3.852` ｜ Yuming Zhang, Xianchen Zhou, Hongxia Wang ｜ National University of Defense Technology

**研究问题**：扩散式组合优化求解器在贪心解码前预测与约束对齐较弱，导致解码跳过与解质量下降。

**方法**：提出免训练的测试时引导PISG，削弱边门控构造结构退化预测并外推，放大约束对齐信号。

**结果与证据**：贪心解码下TSP全部16组求解器-规模对与MIS-ER全部4个求解器均获提升，MIS-ER增益最高+4.8%。

**方向关联**：与进化计算/群智及LLM/API主线无直接交集，仅组合优化问题域与进化算法常用基准相邻，关系较弱。

**代码复现线索**：论文说明PISG免训练但非免调参：尺度与扰动策略需按任务-分布对做小规模验证搜索；两条参数共享分支可在推理时批处理。


<details>
<summary>原始摘要</summary>

> Diffusion-based solvers have become a prominent approach to neural combinatorial optimization. At inference time, they assign each decision variable a prediction score. A greedy decoder ranks candidates by these scores and checks hard constraints to ensure feasibility. Top-ranked candidates should be both promising and jointly feasible, making prediction-constraint alignment critical. When this alignment is weak, greedy decoding skips constraint-violating candidates and falls back to lower-ranked alternatives, degrading solution quality. Existing test-time methods improve search, sampling, or decoding, but do not directly strengthen this alignment before decoding. We observe that trained denoisers already carry constraint-aligned structural signals. Building on this observation, we propose Perturbed Instance-Structure Guidance (PISG), a training-free method that amplifies these signals at test time. At each denoising step, PISG constructs a structure-degraded prediction and extrapolates the unperturbed prediction away from it. The resulting guided prediction improves candidate rankings before constrained decoding. Signal analysis shows that this guidance concentrates on constraint-critical variables rather than uniformly rescaling scores. Under greedy decoding and without downstream refinement, PISG improves all 16 TSP solver-scale pairs across DIFUSCO, T2T, FastT2T, and StruDiCO, and all four MIS-ER solvers, with MIS-ER solved-cost gains up to +4.8%. The gains persist on most large-scale settings after 2-opt, and a preliminary study extends PISG to a Transformer-based solver. These results identify prediction-constraint alignment as an effective test-time enhancement direction for diffusion-based combinatorial optimization. Code is available at https://github.com/Ming-321/PISG.

</details>

## 10. Rethinking Supervision Scaling in On-Policy Distillation: From Dense Supervision to Stochastic Horizons

`相似度 3.783` ｜ Xinhao Yao, Zhenxing Dou, Yong Liu

**研究问题**：研究在线蒸馏中监督效用随轨迹长度的变化，发现逐token密集监督存在收益递减但深层位置仍可学习。

**方法**：提出SH-OPD，按批次随机采样rollout长度以削减冗余监督，刻画重加权偏差并推导计算-重加权权衡。

<details>

Gradient analysis further identifies shared update components across response positions and diverse position-specific residuals whose cross-rollout consistency decreases at greater depths. These results highlight the benefits of efficient supervision allocation across response depths.

## 11. Foundation Pareto Flow Policy for Multi-Objective Reinforcement Learning

`相似度 3.778` ｜ Zhanjiang Yang, Lijun Sun, Yueming Li, Meng Li, Yijun Yang ｜ Shenzhen University; Tsinghua University; Shenzhen Technology University

In this paper, we obtain finite-time bounds for heterogeneous FRL with average rewards. We show that optimal convergence rate and linear speedup are achievable even with a universal stepsize choice, independent of the underlying dynamics. To achieve our result, we modify the existing one-timescale FRL method to a novel two-timescale variant that additionally incorporates iterate averaging. We remark that this idea also extends to other settings already studied in the literature, such as the homogeneous FRL or the heterogeneous case with a discounted cost function.

> Learning the underlying probability distribution from samples is a core problem in statistics and applied mathematics. Generative models based on diffusion and flow matching learn a velocity field whose flow transports a known distribution to the data distribution. They produce novel, realistic samples, although the empirical learning problem is ill posed near the data distribution. Its minimizer transports the samples onto the training data, and small perturbations of the data change the velocity field strongly near the end of the flow. The observed generalization is often attributed to implicit regularization by the network parameterization, early stopping of the training, and the time integration during sampling. Explicit regularization has advanced the theory and computation of many inverse problems and has recently been proposed to prevent memorization in generative models. With a regularizer on the magnitude of the velocity, however, the field approaches the memorizing velocity unless the regularization parameter grows to infinity fast enough, and it then stays bounded by a constant that is small only for large parameters. We show that penalizing the derivatives of order $k$ of the velocity renders the $D$-dimensional empirical problem well posed uniformly in time if $2k > D$ and the training points are in general position. Our experiments in one and two dimensions discretize the velocity with splines and use direct solvers for the regularized least-squares problems to exclude optimization error. Refining the discretization in space and time changes the error of the Hessian-regularized flows only marginally, whereas without the regularizer the error tends to that of memorization. These flows generalize, with errors comparable to or lower than those of well-tuned kernel density estimates.
