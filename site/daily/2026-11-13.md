## 4. Splitting and Alternating Minimization Methods for Training Orthogonal Deep Feedforward Networks

`相似度 3.694` ｜ Jiawei Wang ｜ Hong Kong Baptist University

**研究问题**：训练权重矩阵逐层约束在Stiefel流形上的正交深度前馈网络，克服正交约束与非光滑激活函数带来的优化难题。

**方法**：引入辅助变量将嵌套非光滑结构与流形约束解耦为等式约束优化，提出OrthALM与OrthADMM两种交替最小化算法，各块子问题均有精确闭式解。

**结果与证据**：理论上证明生成序列的聚点为重构问题的KKT点；实验显示OrthALM训练误差与约束残差更低，OrthADMM测试精度相当且多数对比中运行时间最短。

**方向关联**：论文属正交深度网络的ALM/ADMM类训练优化，与档案中进化计算/群智算法及LLM/API两条支线均无直接交集，仅共享优化算法设计这一泛化主题，关联较弱。


<details>
<summary>原始摘要</summary>

> Training deep feedforward networks with weight matrices constrained to layer-wise Stiefel manifolds serves as an effective regularization technique to improve generalization. However, the simultaneous presence of orthogonality constraints and nonsmooth activation functions poses severe theoretical and computational challenges for standard stochastic gradient and Riemannian optimization methods. In this paper, we propose two splitting and alternating-based algorithms to train orthogonal deep feedforward networks (OrthDFNs). By introducing auxiliary variables, we decouple the nested nonsmooth compositions and manifold constraints into a structured equality-constrained optimization problem. To solve this model, we develop OrthALM, an inexact augmented Lagrangian method (ALM) that employs a proximal alternating minimization scheme to solve its subproblems. Crucially, every block coordinate subproblem admits an exact, closed-form solution. We establish a rigorous convergence theory for OrthALM, proving that every accumulation point of the generated sequence is a Karush--Kuhn--Tucker (KKT) point of the regularized, reformulated problem. Furthermore, we introduce OrthADMM as a practical, proximal-free variant that executes a single inner alternating sweep per multiplier update to accelerate computation. Extensive numerical experiments show that OrthALM attains lower training error and constraint residuals than OrthADMM in most configurations. OrthADMM achieves competitive test accuracy and the lowest reported runtime in most comparisons with the selected baselines.

## 9. Stability of equilibria in discrete-time modified-gradient systems and the multivariate breeder's equation

`相似度 3.497` ｜ Benjamin J. Ridenhour ｜ University of Idaho

**研究问题**：研究离散时间修正梯度系统（多变量育种方程）平衡点的稳定性，检验连续时间稳定性结论能否推广到离散迭代。

**方法**：用一维二次反例证明直接类比失效，再借单步Lyapunov引理（离散下降引理）证明步长界下的稳定性，并论证各假设必要性。

**结果与证据**：证明步长界λ\_n\(P\_k\)≤2/L恢复一致稳定性，更严界加∑λ\_1\(P\_k\)=∞恢复一致渐近稳定性，两界精确且各假设必要。

**方向关联**：与进化计算支线有具体交集：论文将稳定性条件应用于高斯进化优化算法（进化策略）的均值更新，属进化算法理论分析；与LLM/API主线无直接关联。


<details>
<summary>原始摘要</summary>

> The multivariate breeder's equation $Δ\bar z=G\nabla\ln\bar W$ is a widely used discrete-time modified-gradient system describing the evolution of continuous traits in organisms. This system could more conventionally be expressed as $x_{k+1}=x_k+P_k\nabla f(x_k)$. For its continuous-time counterpart $x'=P(t)\nabla f(x)$, it has been proven that an isolated local maximum of $f$ is uniformly stable whenever $P(t)$ is positive semidefinite, as is the case for the "$G$-matrix." Furthermore, the continuous time system is uniformly asymptotically stable when the maximum is an isolated critical point and $\int_0^\inftyλ_1(P(t))\,dt=\infty$. We show that the literal discrete analog of this result is false: with constant positive definite $P$ the iterates can oscillate or diverge. The result is restored by a single additional hypothesis, a uniform bound on the largest eigenvalue of $P_k$, $λ_n$, such that $λ_n(P_k)\le 2/L$ where $L$ is a local Lipschitz constant of $\nabla f$. Under this bound the maximum is uniformly stable; under a stricter version together with $\sum_kλ_1(P_k)=\infty$, the maximum is uniformly asymptotically stable. Given these hypotheses, a level-set estimate of the basin of attraction carries over. Both bounds are sharp, and each remaining hypothesis is shown to be necessary. Biologically, the bound says that genetic variance in every direction must be small relative to the curvature of log mean fitness; outside this regime the mean phenotype can overshoot a fitness peak as hypothesized by Fisher's geometric model. As further application of our results, the same conditions apply to the mean update of Gaussian evolutionary optimization algorithms, in which the covariance of the search population plays the role of $G$.

</details>

## 10. Early Signals, Strong Decisions: Prefix-Guided Sampling for Parallel Test-Time Scaling

**结果与证据**：论文实验称 PreG 在多个推理基准上一致优于 DeepConf、Self-Certainty 等标准并行 TTS 方法，并显著降低 token 用量与整体计算。

<summary>原始摘要</summary>

> Parallel test-time scaling (TTS) has been shown to enhance reasoning in large language models by generating and aggregating multiple independent reasoning paths. Internal confidence scores can further improve this process by identifying paths of varying quality. However, existing methods remain computationally inefficient, as low-quality paths are fully expanded before being evaluated. In this work, we aim to improve the use of internal confidence signals for better model performance and computational efficiency. We begin by offering a new perspective on the role of confidence in aggregation, showing that it is most impactful for questions near the decision boundary. Building on this insight, we introduce Prefix-Guided Sampling (PreG), a method that reallocates computation by first generating short prefixes, ranking them using internal confidence scores, and then completing only the most promising candidates. We provide theoretical analysis demonstrating that, under a fixed compute budget, PreG strengthens the effective signal by widening the decision boundary margin. Empirically, our method consistently outperforms standard parallel TTS approaches across benchmarks, while significantly improving token efficiency and reducing overall computation.
