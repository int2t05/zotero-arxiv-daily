## 2. Accelerating Dynamic Route Re-solving via Robust Solver Evolution in Automatic Heuristic Design

**研究问题**：LLM自动启发式设计在静态TSP实例上进化解算器，执行中请求动态变更后重求解低效，源于静态过拟合。

<details>
<summary>原始摘要</summary>

> Multi-goal route planning uses spatial data on service locations and pairwise travel costs to determine a visiting order that minimizes total travel cost. This task can be formulated as the traveling salesman problem (TSP) and has benefited from large language model-based automatic heuristic design (AHD) for constructing effective solvers. Despite their effectiveness in static settings, existing methods exhibit substantial re-solving inefficiency when location changes, new requests, or request cancellations alter the underlying request data during execution. Our empirical analysis suggests that ``static-overfitting'' contributes to this inefficiency, as evolved solvers may repeatedly direct search toward overlapping local neighborhoods after dynamic changes. We further find that this ``overfitting'' is associated with limitations in existing evolutionary processes, where lower objective values on some instances coincide with longer solving times. In this paper, we therefore propose ERDR, a plug-and-play framework that integrates two strategies into existing AHD pipelines to guide evolution toward solvers that re-solve dynamically changed instances faster. Specifically, 1) ERDR uses validation-guided reselection to reevaluate candidates on instances excluded from routine evolution, reducing specialization to fixed training instances. 2) ERDR applies runtime robustness-augmented evaluation, supplementing the original evaluation objective of AHD methods with inter-group variability and tail risk in solving time to discourage costly and uneven search. Experiments across multiple TSP scales and dynamic scenarios show that ERDR achieves average re-solving speedups of $2.2\times$ under guided local search and $4.4\times$ under iterated local search.

## 4. Sampling at Scale: Distributed Multi-Agent Trajectory Optimization

`相似度 4.040` ｜ Minchan Jung, Dimitrios S. Georgiou, Augustinos D. Saravanos, Evangelos A. Theodorou ｜ Massachusetts Institute of Technology; Georgia Institute of Technology

**研究问题**：采样式方法在多智能体轨迹优化中难以随维度扩展，分布式集成时需兼顾约束导致的非凸探索与大规模可扩展性。

**方法**：提出DS-TO分布式采样轨迹优化架构：分裂机制使各智能体仅在本体控制空间采样，完全去中心化，并配合Jacobi并行更新与单步约束线性化。

**结果与证据**：2D与3D任务、最多1024个智能体的实验表明：相比先进导数式分布式优化探索与约束满足更好，比现有分布式采样方法扩展性更强。

**方向关联**：与辅助阅读方向'分布式多智能体计算'及群智算法的采样式非凸探索思想有具体重叠；但与LLM/API主线无关，仅适合作辅助阅读。


<details>
<summary>原始摘要</summary>

> Multi-agent trajectory optimization poses two coupled challenges: effectively exploring non-convex landscapes and scaling favorably to large teams of agents. Sampling-based methods are natural candidates for the exploration challenge but scale poorly with problem dimension. Moreover, their off-the-shelf integration into distributed optimization introduces a fundamental trade-off: sampling either fails to address the constraint-induced non-convexity or its search space grows with neighborhood size. We address this gap by introducing a $\underline{D}\text{istributed}$ $\underline{S}\text{ampling-based}$ $\underline{T}\text{rajectory}$ $\underline{O}\text{ptimization} \ \text{(DS-TO)}$ architecture built on a splitting mechanism tailored to sampling. Under this splitting, each agent samples only in its own control space, independent of neighborhood size, while still remaining exposed to both the constraint-induced non-convexity and the decisions of other agents. The resulting algorithm is fully decentralized, requiring only neighborhood communication, with all local updates executed in parallel across agents. Efficient variations, including parallel Jacobi updates within each agent's subproblem and single-step constraint linearization, further reduce computation. Experiments on 2D and 3D tasks with up to 1024 agents demonstrate improved exploration and constraint satisfaction over state-of-the-art derivative-based distributed optimization, and superior scalability to existing distributed sampling methods.

</details>

<details>
