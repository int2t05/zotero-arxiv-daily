## 1. Simplicity Goes Far: Auditing Prompt Optimizers with Demonstration-Conditioned Prompt Search

`相似度 4.411` ｜ Bohe Chen ｜ Queen Mary University of London; Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications

**研究问题**：复杂提示优化器的收益究竟来自策略更新、反思与进化等机制，还是仅来自生成加验证集选择的简单步骤？

**方法**：提出DCPS四阶段控制：冻结生成器、按随机采样的示例生成候选提示、在验证集评分、择优保留，不含更新、反思或进化。

**结果与证据**：StablePrompt中DCPS替代PPO精度差距0.5pp内、成本约三分之一；匹配GEPA精度时rollout少55%、API成本降32%。

**方向关联**：直接契合LLM与进化计算交互重点：审计GEPA等反思与Pareto进化机制的边际价值；机制归因与成本报告亦契合科研方法主线。

**代码复现线索**：正文详述四阶段流程与两种实例化（Top-K与贪心替换），并给出StablePrompt数据角色安排与沿用GEPA的rollout协议。

<summary>原始摘要</summary>

> Automatic prompt optimization is growing more elaborate. Recent methods stack policy-gradient updates, trace reflection, and evolutionary search. These methods share a generate-and-select step; without controlling for it, reported gains may conflate the contributions of optimizer mechanisms and larger sampling budgets. We isolate this step as Demonstration-Conditioned Prompt Search (DCPS), repurposing the Automatic Prompt Engineer (APE) recipe as a mechanism-attribution control: it freezes the generator, conditions it on randomly sampled demonstrations, scores candidates on validation data, and keeps the best---no learned update, reflection, or evolution. Across two optimizer families, DCPS replaces Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO) in StablePrompt, matching classification accuracy within 0.5 pp at roughly one-third the measured cost; in DSPy-based compound AI systems, under the reported budgets, it matches GEPA's macro accuracy on GPT-4.1-mini while using 55% fewer rollouts at 32% lower measured API cost on average. The audit reveals a task-dependent pattern: proposal and selection recover much of heavier optimizers' performance on structured and instruction-following tasks, while additional mechanisms retain advantages in some open-ended and reasoning-heavy settings. We recommend benchmarking prompt optimizers against generate-and-select controls and reporting rollout counts, token usage, and dollar costs alongside accuracy. Code and artifacts: https://github.com/bhc6/DCPS.

## 2. Evolving Neural Solvers for Multi-objective Combinatorial Optimization

`相似度 4.183` ｜ Ya-Hui Jia, Le-Xin Wen, Yang Wang, Wei-Neng Chen, Yi Mei

**研究问题**：神经求解多目标组合优化时，专用偏好模型训练成本高，共享模型又易对不同偏好输出重复解。

**方法**：提出ENS：先在估计拐点偏好处训练模型，经参数迁移向极端偏好得到初始模型集，再用进化策略演化出更多能产生非支配解的求解器。

**结果与证据**：作者报告：在经典MOCO问题上，ENS在多数评估设置中的超体积指标优于最先进的神经MOCO方法。

**方向关联**：与进化计算基础方向契合：用进化策略演化神经求解器，属神经与进化计算交互，但论文不涉及LLM/API。


<details>
<summary>原始摘要</summary>

> Multi-objective combinatorial optimization (MOCO) seeks high-quality solutions that represent different trade-offs among conflicting objectives. Existing neural methods typically train a dedicated model for each preference or condition a single model on different preferences. Training dedicated models becomes more costly as the number of preferences increases, while a shared model can produce duplicate solutions for different preferences, limiting the benefit of denser preference queries. We propose the Evolving Neural Solver (ENS), which trains only a small set of preference-specific models and takes advantage of evolutionary computation to evolve them into a diverse collection of solvers for better solution quality and Pareto front coverage. ENS first trains a model at an estimated knee-point preference to target a balanced compromise, then obtains the remaining initial models through parameter transfer toward the extremes, so as to preliminarily approximate the Pareto front. It then evolves these models to find more candidates that can generate non-dominated solutions through evolution strategies. Experimental results on classic MOCO problems show that ENS outperforms state-of-the-art neural MOCO methods in terms of hypervolume in most evaluated settings.

</details>

## 3. Learning Optimal Transport Plans Via Autoregressive Token Regression

**研究问题**：研究自回归token回归能否让transformer学习求解最优传输这类有真值解、有稀疏结构的约束优化问题。

<summary>原始摘要</summary>

> Optimal Transport (OT) provides a principled framework for finding the minimum cost coupling between probability distributions. Classical solvers remain the gold standard for this task. At the same time, OT is an ideal testbed for studying whether learning-based amortized models can internalize the structure of OT type problems. Here, ground-truth solutions are available and marginal constraints are explicitly defined. Also, exact discrete solutions have well-characterized sparsity. We describe ToR-OT, a transformer-based model that learns to predict discrete OT plans through autoregressive token regression. We reframe OT into a structured sequence generation task. ToR-OT decodes sparse transport plans as sequences instead of solving an optimization task at test time. Overall, ToR-OT is not yet a replacement for classical solvers, but a step toward understanding how autoregressive transformers can learn structure of constrained optimization problems like OT.

## 4. Towards Optimism-Pessimism Trade-off in Model-based Offline-to-Online Reinforcement Learning

**结果与证据**：D4RL连续控制基准上，HiP3在同任务与新在线任务平均性能最强，更强分布偏移下仍保持优势，消融厘清策略池、构建与在线选择的作用。

**方向关联**：与进化计算支线存在方法层面交集：参考向量、帕累托前沿构建与邻域搜索属多目标优化思想；但论文本身是强化学习，不含进化算法或LLM成分。

<summary>原始摘要</summary>

> Model-based offline-to-online reinforcement learning (RL) enables sample-efficient adaptation by leveraging offline pre-training for online fine-tuning. However, the distribution shifts between offline and online stages often hinder fine-tuning performance. Many existing methods encode the optimism-pessimism trade-off through a single-objective formulation. Selecting its trade-off coefficient through online fine-tuning can require costly bi-level optimization. We identify the balance established during offline training as a key challenge: in our motivating experiments, optimistic policies adapt better to novel tasks, whereas pessimistic policies perform better on the same online tasks. To address this challenge, we propose a bi-objective formulation that captures this trade-off, yielding a Pareto policy pool during offline training. After offline training, a contextual bandit algorithm hierarchically selects the most suitable policy for fine-tuning at each online interaction step. Empirically, our pipeline, Hierarchical Pareto Policy Pool (HiP3), achieves the strongest aggregate performance across both same and novel online tasks and maintains its advantage under stronger distribution shifts. Comprehensive ablations further clarify the roles of the policy pool, MOSAIC-based pool construction, and the online selection mechanism.

**代码复现线索**：论文明确声明其带精确边界条件的PINN实现代码已开源，但正文未给出实现、数据或实验设置的其他具体细节。

<summary>原始摘要</summary>

> We use neural networks to optimize trajectories by modeling the position as a function of time and minimizing the thrust with respect to the network parameters. This corresponds to an unsupervised training procedure in which physical laws are incorporated directly into the optimization objective. In standard physics-informed neural networks, initial and terminal states are enforced through additional weighted loss terms, resulting in a multi-objective optimization problem that is highly sensitive to the choice of loss weights and can lead to poor convergence and reduced accuracy. To address this challenge, we construct a simple transformation, exploiting the mathematical structure of trajectory optimization problems, to enforce boundary conditions exactly a-priori. This transformation can be interpreted as a reparameterization of the solution space that embeds the boundary conditions directly into the function. This converts the original constrained optimization problem into an unconstrained one over admissible trajectories. I.e. the transformation eliminates the boundary condition penalty terms and reduces the problem to a single-objective optimization. Crucially, trajectory optimization comes with a low-dimensional boundary, which allows particularly simple and stable constructions of distance functions compared to general domains in partial differential equations. Numerical experiments demonstrate that the proposed approach meaningfully improves convergence behavior and solution accuracy.

## 6. High-Dimensional Black-Box Optimization via Guidance Sampling of Diffusion Models

`相似度 3.836` ｜ Xiaoyan Hu, Rohit Sonker, Jalal Butt, Jiayu Chen, Egemen Kolemen, Jeff Schneider

**研究问题**：在评估次数有限的高维黑箱优化中，现有扩散模型优化方法依赖标注数据或奖励梯度，适用性受限。

**方法**：提出BReaD框架：以高斯过程作未知目标的可微代理并主动查询标签，用UCB作引导信号驱动扩散模型采样以平衡探索与利用。

**结果与证据**：论文给出生成分布与目标分布总变差距离的理论分析；科学发现与图像生成实验显示低数据下可优化黑箱奖励。

**方向关联**：与进化计算支线有具体重叠：少评估高维黑箱优化、GP代理与探索利用权衡和代理辅助进化算法的课题设定相近；但方法为扩散采样而非进化算法，与LLM/API支线关联弱。


<details>
<summary>原始摘要</summary>

> Many real-world optimization problems involve optimizing black-box objectives for which only a limited number of evaluations are available. Bayesian optimization (BO) is a powerful framework for such problems, but its effectiveness often deteriorates in high-dimensional spaces. Recent work leverages diffusion models for black-box optimization, often through pre-training a reward-conditioned score network to steer generation towards high-reward samples. Alternatively, gradient-based guidance methods assume access to a differentiable reward function from which guidance gradients are queried. However, these approaches either require large amounts of labeled data for offline training or access to exact reward gradients, limiting their applicability in black-box settings. We propose BReaD (Black-box Reward-guided Sampling of Diffusion Model), a guidance sampling framework for optimizing black-box rewards without assuming access to a labeled dataset or reward gradients. The proposed method uses a Gaussian process (GP) as a differentiable surrogate for the unknown objective and actively queries labels for the generated samples. We provide a theoretical analysis of the total-variation distance between the generated distribution and the target distribution. Using Upper Confidence Bound (UCB) as guidance signals, our method balances exploration and exploitation during generation and enables the discovery of high-reward regions in the data space. Numerical experiments on scientific discovery and image generation tasks demonstrate that our framework can effectively optimize black-box rewards in low-data scenarios.

</details>

## 100. Residual-Guided Lightweight Reinforcement Learning for Risk-Aware Trajectory Selection with a Frozen World Model

`相似度 3.019` ｜ Jiaqi Wu, Yongqi Dong, Yijie Zhong, Maple Shao, Yide Tao, Linsen Cai et al. ｜ Monash University; RWTH Aachen University

**研究问题**：自动驾驶中世界模型可预测未来场景，但固定的轨迹选择规则难以捕捉场景相关的权衡。

**方法**：提出残差引导RL框架：冻结世界模型与固定候选库，用PPO微调候选选择与有界残差的联合策略，部署时仅保留约1.2万参数的排序网络。

**结果与证据**：nuScenes开环验证集上L2较RiskWorld基线降3.99%；同等预算下残差探索较无残差PPO再降0.83%，风险特征移除后仍保持更低L2。

**方向关联**：该文属强化学习与自动驾驶轨迹选择，与进化计算/群智算法及LLM/API支线无直接重叠，相关性弱。

<summary>原始摘要</summary>

> In automated driving, world models provide future scene predictions for evaluating candidate trajectories, but fixed selection rules may not capture scene-dependent trade-offs. We propose a residual-guided reinforcement learning (RL) framework for lightweight trajectory selection with a frozen world model and a fixed candidate bank. The framework uses proximal policy optimization (PPO) to fine-tune a joint policy over candidate selection and bounded trajectory residuals. A context-conditioned ranking network learns candidate preferences from feedback obtained by evaluating local perturbations of the selected trajectories. At inference, the selector combines learned preference and predicted risk ranks using fixed weights and returns the selected candidate trajectory without modification. Fine-tuning updates 16,106 parameters across the actor and critic, while the deployed selector retains only the 11,725-parameter ranking network, with the residual branch and critic used exclusively during training. In open-loop evaluation on the nuScenes validation set, the method reduces average Euclidean trajectory error (L2) by 3.99% relative to the RiskWorld model baseline without RL-based selection. Under matched budgets for feedback accesses and actor updates, residual exploration reduces L2 by 0.83% relative to the categorical PPO variant trained without residuals. The method also maintains lower L2 than RiskWorld when risk features are removed or shuffled.
