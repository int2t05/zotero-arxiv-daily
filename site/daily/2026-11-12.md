## 1. Specializing Meta Multi-Objectivization as Baseline for Software Engineering Optimization

`相似度 4.254` ｜ Chengjie Chuan, Pengzhou Chen, Tao Chen

**研究问题**：软件工程优化问题易陷局部最优；MMO原仅用于配置调优，本文研究其能否推广为通用基线及辅助目标如何选择。

**方法**：理论上分析辅助目标选择的关键性，在9个SE优化问题共212案例上实证MMO，并提出L2RAC工具自动推荐辅助目标。

**结果与证据**：据论文：选对辅助目标时MMO在79%/94%案例分别优于SOTA与通用优化器；L2RAC排序准确度至多提升60%，省99%试错成本。

<summary>原始摘要</summary>

> Given the discrete nature of many software engineering (SE) optimization problems, trapping at local optima remains a crucial issue in the optimizer designs. Meta Multi-Objectivization (MMO) is an optimization model for overcoming local optima by introducing an extra, auxiliary objective. Yet, MMO is originally designed only for configuration tuning, leaving its suitability for other SE problems in doubt, particularly regarding the choice of the auxiliary objective. In this paper, we study the possibility and resolution for specializing MMO as a general baseline across a range of SE optimization problems, making it a fundamental counterpart for future SE optimizers that seek to mitigate the issue of local optima. We do so by providing both theoretical remit and empirical evidence: we analytically show how the choice of the auxiliary objective is crucial while demonstrating the effectiveness of MMO under nine SE optimization problems comprising 212 cases when the right one can be chosen, i.e., MMO is able to outperform the other state-of-the-art and general-purpose optimizers in 79% and 94% of the cases, respectively. Drawing on the theoretical understanding and empirical findings, we then propose L2RAC, a tool that automatically recommends the best auxiliary objective choice for a given SE problem/case, hence helping to specialize MMO therein. Through extensive experiments, we reveal that, in general, L2RAC can more accurately rank the choice of auxiliary objective for MMO under a case than other guesses by up to 60%, saving as much as 99% of the cost that would otherwise be required to conduct trial-and-error when using MMO.

<details>
<summary>原始摘要</summary>

> We introduce LongTails, a framework for discovering and improving oracle-complexity bounds with formal verification in Lean 4. This turns the search for new methods into an ongoing effort to improve their theoretical guarantees. To the best of our knowledge, LongTails is the first framework to scale automated research in optimization across such a broad range of problems with formally verified theoretical guarantees.

<summary>原始摘要</summary>

## 7. A Fast Proximal-Gradient Algorithm for Decentralised Optimisation

**研究问题**：去中心化多智能体网络中，各代理仅知局部光滑与非光滑凸函数分量，需仅凭邻居通信协作最小化有限和凸问题。

<details>
<summary>原始摘要</summary>

> We consider a connected multi-agent network where agents coordinate to minimise a finite sum of convex functions.
